Story preview · Spoiler-free

Blackthorne is keeping secrets.

Mara Ellery arrives searching for answers about her sister’s death. But the university’s old halls hold more than history: hidden alliances, unsettling bargains, and the presence of Adrian Vale, whose secrets may be bound to the truth she needs.

As attraction and suspicion pull in opposite directions, one mystery looms over everything: the Ninth Door. What lies behind it—and why is someone so determined to keep it closed?
